<!-- title: i've realized that i don't like video games anymore -->
<!-- subtitle: most of them, anyways -->

that's probably a loaded title to lead with. i play plenty of video games, i care about the medium as an artistic expression, i value the games both big and small that capture my imagination, and i'd probably be pretty sad if i knew that a video game would never again make me feel that way. i guess more specifically, i've realized that i don't care about the marketable ideal of what a video game is now.

obviously these feelings have been dragged out by the recent debacle with the game awards nominating grand theft auto 6 in every possible category before the game is even out, and to a lesser extent, the glowing reviews that the game is receiving across the board. i am a bit of a snobbish asshole, but i see this and i roll my eyes. when has a gta game ever provoked any kind of introspection or extrospection? when has ever relayed its themes to me besides "the world and everyone in it is an asshole"? why is this the game that will "move gaming forward"?

when i was a kid, i was easy to win over. most kids are! if you dangle a sufficiently shiny collection of keys in front of a kid enough, you'll probably have enough market interest to slap a $19.99 price tag on it. as i've gotten older, my tastes matured, my interests shifted, and i guess the things that used to enrapture me then don't really do the same now. even the games i used to love, like ocarina of time and pokemon, have become technical fascinations, a thing to pick apart so i can see the gears inside turn -- rather than an imagined world to get lost in. 

what part of gta captures anyone's imagination anymore? maybe i'm being a little presumptuous, but i think it'll be a game where you steal cars and shoot people. it's not going to be fine art, a technical achievement sure, but at what cost? is art anything without the people making it? is it logistical for something with the mass market appeal of grand theft auto to have any sense of taste or themes at all?

games like gta6 will probably win every award it was nominated for at the game awards while games like mewgenics and unbeatable -- games with outstanding soundtracks made individual people with a vision -- don't even get a best score nomination, or acknowledgement. awards shows are always spiritually dead, it's obvious that keighly's got a stick in his ass about being the academy awards for video games, but the entire thing reeks of insincerity. in that way it's really hard to care about it. 

everyone loves video games now. it has never been a better time to "get into" games. they're huge, mass market products made to sell to millions of people, with a market share bigger than hollywood -- and that's my frustration. the top of the line market moving games are too big to have independent visions behind them. they inspire nothing in me. dan houser didn't make gta6, aonuma didn't make ocarina of time 2026... i miss feeling a creative's fingerprints in their work. i miss when games like pokemon were so scrappy they couldn't even afford to get the move effects working without a slew of bugs. yeah, games were lower budget, messier, complicated things... but you wouldn't correct mistakes in the lines of the mona lisa, right?

i will probably eventually play oot 2026 and gta6. they're interesting for no other reason than to see how they compare to their predescesors. they're probably still *good* games, but i am in no rush to participate launch day theatrics so that i can jerk off another company crushing the artists making their products under their own boots.

anyways, my ass is not buying a ps5 or switch 2, lol.
